# EduPilot-AI

## 🚀 AI-Powered Student Assistant

**EduPilot-AI** is an intelligent student companion designed to help students learn smarter, plan better, prepare for exams, track progress, and build their careers — all in one platform.

Students often use different platforms for doubt solving, notes, study planning, exam preparation, and career guidance. EduPilot-AI brings these experiences together into one personalized and student-friendly platform.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 **AI Study Assistant** – Get instant help with academic questions and difficult concepts.
- 📚 **Smart Study Planner** – Create personalized study plans based on goals, subjects, and available time.
- 📝 **AI Study Kit** – Generate summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and important questions from study material.
- 🎯 **Personalized Learning** – Receive learning recommendations based on progress and performance.
- 💼 **Career Mentor** – Explore career paths, required skills, internships, and preparation strategies.
- 📊 **Progress Tracking** – Track study goals, completed tasks, quiz performance, and learning progress.
- 🌐 **Student Dashboard** – Access learning, planning, career, and progress tools from one place.

## 💡 Problem Statement

Students face difficulty managing multiple academic and career-related activities across different platforms.

EduPilot-AI solves this problem by providing a **single intelligent platform for learning, planning, exam preparation, and career development.**

## 🌟 Vision

Our vision is to create a personalized digital learning companion that helps every student:

**Learn Smarter. Plan Better. Grow Faster. 🚀**
